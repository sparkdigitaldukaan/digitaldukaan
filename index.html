How are first-trial prices calculated?+

The site keeps each configured rate as the original price and calculates the 50% discount automatically for the first trial only. Custom quotes are not eligible. Please enquire to confirm trial eligibility.

Are these prices final?+

These are indicative prices. The final quote depends on the agreed scope. We will confirm the price and inclusions with you before any work begins.

Do I need to pay for hosting or a domain?+

A basic static site can be hosted on some free platforms. A custom domain, paid hosting, subscriptions and other third-party costs may be separate. We will explain any external costs before you commit.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

When you submit the enquiry form, Digital Dukaan receives the name, business name, phone number, optional email, requested service and project description you provide. We use these details to understand, respond to and manage your enquiry.

Form details are stored in an access-restricted online system used to manage enquiries. After the save succeeds, they are placed in a WhatsApp message draft so you can contact us; the message is not sent unless you review it and press Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp processes messages under its own terms and privacy practices.

Please do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. You may ask us to correct or delete your enquiry by completing the enquiry form and describing your request. We retain enquiry information only as needed to respond and manage the enquiry, subject to applicable requirements.

Terms & Conditions

Every enquiry must be submitted using the website form and include agreement to the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. The enquiry is accepted into our system only after the form is valid and the database save succeeds.

Submitting an enquiry is a request for information, not an order or service agreement. Digital Dukaan will confirm availability, scope, price, timing and any external costs with you before work begins. No payment is taken through this enquiry form.

After the enquiry is saved, WhatsApp opens a draft for you to review and send. The enquiry is not delivered through WhatsApp unless you press Send. Please provide accurate contact details and do not submit unlawful or sensitive material.
